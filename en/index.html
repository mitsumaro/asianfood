About

Asian Hatbazaar & Food is a shop where you can enjoy Asian cuisine and goods. From fragrant, spice-filled dishes to unique items inspired by local markets, we share the warm, lively atmosphere of Asia. Enjoy the flavors, the finds, and the time itself—bringing the charm of Asian food and daily life to Japan.

OUR VALUES Our Values

Rich & Flavorful meat

Carefully seasoned and grilled to bring out the meat's natural umami. Fragrant, juicy, and easy to enjoy.

Homemade Original Sauce

All sauces are homemade, with a variety of flavors and spice levels. Choose your favorite and enjoy a taste that’s a little different from any others.

Easy-to-Enjoy Kebabs

Hatbazaar
